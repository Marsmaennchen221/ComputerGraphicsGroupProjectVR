# Best Group

## Gameplay

You spawn inside a room with multiple Assets to interact with.

## Control

The scene can be controlled either with a VR headset and controllers or with keyboard and mouse using the XR Device Simulator.

Keyboard and mouse:
- WASD: Move around the scene.
- Q and E: Move up and down.
- Mouse: Look around.
- T: Control the simulated VR controller.
- Point the controller ray at an object, then press and hold G to grab and hold the object.

VR controls:
- Headset: Look around naturally.
- Left joystick: Move around the room.
- Right joystick: Turn the player view.
- Controller ray: Point at interactable objects.
- Grab button: Press and hold to grab and move objects.
